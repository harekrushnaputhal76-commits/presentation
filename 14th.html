Terms & Policies

1. Technology Platform Only

This App operates strictly as a technology platform connecting independent drivers with riders. We do not own vehicles or employ drivers directly.

2. Mandatory Compliance

All Captains must possess and carry a valid Driving License, Registration Certificate, Active Motor Insurance, and PUC certificate while actively taking rides.

3. Helmet & Traffic Safety

For two-wheeler Captains, wearing an ISI-certified helmet is strictly mandatory. Overspeeding or jumping red lights will lead to instant account termination.

4. Zero Tolerance Policy

We maintain a strict zero-tolerance policy against physical abuse or harassment. Any violation against a Rider will result in permanent suspension.

5. Data Privacy (DPDP Act)

The Captain must keep the Rider's personal information completely confidential. Contacting the Rider outside of the active trip is a severe legal offense.
